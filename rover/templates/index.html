SWITCH TO SAMSUNG HOTSPOT (OPTIONAL):

The rover operates seamlessly in Direct Wi-Fi Mode. To migrate it to your Samsung hotspot instead, follow these 4 steps:

  1. 1
    Power ON the RoboMaster EP rover.
  2. 2
    Slide the connection switch on the intelligent controller to Router Mode (the icon showing networked nodes, not the direct Wi-Fi phone icon).
  3. 3
    Point the rover's front camera directly at the QR code on the left.
  4. 4
    Press the small connection button next to the switch once. You will hear the rover chime as it connects to Samsung!

HOW 2D MAPPING WORKS

Drive the rover around the room using WASD or the on-screen joystick. The front ToF sensor scans distances 10 times a second and continuously projects obstacle surfaces onto the room coordinate map using chassis odometry and yaw attitude.
