FAQs

Frequently
asked questions

Does Grok fly the drone?
No. Grok plans the mission and picks the device and target. A fast local loop handles movement, so the model is never in the control loop.
Who confirms a target?
The operator. Grok proposes a target from a frame with numbered boxes and explains why; nothing follows a person until a human confirms.
Does it use face recognition?
No. Targets are matched by description, such as clothing, not by identifying faces.
What does STOP do?
STOP is a direct control that goes to the device layer without asking the model.
Which devices work today?
The DJI RoboMaster TT and a simulator. Rovers, cameras and other devices are roadmap items.
Is mission control connected to real hardware?
The page linked from this site is a simulation with local sample data. It does not connect to devices or call any API.
